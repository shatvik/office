Process Medicare Claims on Health Rules Payor (HRP) application, Claims are data captured or received electronically and available in Health Rules Payor (HRP). The claim contains information about member, provider, services provided, and corresponding charges claimed. The processor checks eligibility, provider contracts and authorizations. The processor checks for standard applicable rates, state mandates, exceptions and takes appropriate action of either paying the claim, denying with appropriate reasoning, escalating the claim for more information, or transferring it per guidelines. The processor refers to the number of applications to validate information contained on the claim and references e-Policies to process the claim. I
